Teacher's simple future rule

The stem is normally the infinitive. For verbs ending -re, remove the final -e; add -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont. Examples: je voyagerai, tu prendras, elle verra, nous irons.

Important irregular stems: avoir → aur-, être → ser-, aller → ir-, faire → fer-, devoir → devr-, envoyer → enverr-, pouvoir → pourr-, recevoir → recevr-, savoir → saur-, venir → viendr-, voir → verr-, vouloir → voudr-.

Perfect tense and sophisticated phrases

J'ai mangé (avoir + past participle), je suis allée (être and agreement), elles sont sorties, je me suis amusée, je n'ai pas aimé. Practise the agreement that matches the subject.

Useful dictation phrases: le mois dernier, l'année dernière, pendant lequel, ce qui était, même si, néanmoins, incontournable, impressionnant(e), il y a beaucoup de choses à voir et à faire, j'espère, nous irons.

How to award AQA Higher Q3 marks

AO2 is a holistic judgement of relevance, idea development and how clearly you communicate. Both bullet points should be covered. If only one bullet is addressed, the AQA AO2 maximum is 12/15.

AO2 bandGuidance
13–15Substantial relevant detail, ideas regularly developed and communication very clear
10–12Good amount of relevant detail, mostly successful development and generally clear meaning
7–9Adequate detail, some developed points, occasional lapses in clarity
4–6Limited development; information sometimes hard to understand
1–3Very limited content and frequent communication problems

AO3 Range (0–5): assess varied appropriate vocabulary and how well longer or complex sentences work. AO3 Accuracy (0–5): assess grammatical errors, correct verb forms and security of tenses throughout. A score of AO2 = 0 requires AO3 = 0 under AQA's criteria.
