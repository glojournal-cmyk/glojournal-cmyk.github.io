How your practice is recorded

Guided questions give you a sentence or grammatical structure. Their results are kept separately from independent answers. This practice does not award Daily points or original-question mastery. A small subset of independent finite-verb items can also be installed into the existing topic question banks; the main Daily planner must be separately tested before claiming those items are scheduled.

An unfamiliar answer stays pending, with any confirmed marks kept. Rephrase the flagged box, or export your answers for review. Pending answers do not count as finished. Showing an example marks the attempt as assisted.

Year groups follow the existing bank. Your teacher’s current topic list takes priority.
