## Source defects discovered and corrected in this staging PR

1. `phys-y8-pressure-density-fluids-density-equation-apply`: calculated density of 540 g / 200 cm³ is **2.7 g/cm³**, not 3. Corrected source `value`, `modelAnswer` and valid density units to match existing `markPoints` and feedback.
2. `phys-y8-moments-levers-moment-equation-apply`: moment is **10 N m**, not 10 N. Corrected model answer and accepted dimensions.
3. `phys-y8-moments-levers-direction-apply`: 4 N m + 4 N m clockwise is **8 N m clockwise**, not 8 N; corrected units/model answer, with two mark points still separately reviewed.

**Grading safeguard:** The objective scorer formerly accepted a correct *final value* as full credit on two-/three-mark numeric items. This could incorrectly give 2/2 or 3/3 when working, direction or an explanation had not been supplied. It now returns `reviewRequired:true`, `marksEarned:null`, `correct:null` on all 29 multi-mark numeric items, even when the final number and unit are right. Do not infer actual marks without a separately reviewed method/explanation rubric. One-mark, exact-objective answers can still produce read-only previews.
