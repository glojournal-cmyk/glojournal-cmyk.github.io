Balanced diet and nutrients

A balanced diet supplies the right amount of energy and all essential nutrients for health, growth and repair.

Overview

A balanced diet supplies the right amount of energy and all essential nutrients for health, growth and repair.

Watch for

Calling fibre a nutrient that releases energy. Forgetting water or minerals when listing all seven components. Giving a food example when the question asks for a function.

Must remember

  • ✓Recall the precise Year 8 vocabulary for balanced diet and nutrients.
  • ✓Apply the knowledge rather than relying on recognition alone.
  • ✓Use the command word and include every required scientific point.
  • ✓Seven components: carbohydrate, protein, lipid, fibre, vitamins, minerals and water.
  • ✓Carbohydrate is the main respiration fuel; protein supports growth and repair; lipids store energy and insulate.
  • ✓Starch becomes simple sugars, protein becomes amino acids, and lipid becomes fatty acids plus glycerol.

Watch for

  • Calling fibre a nutrient that releases energy.
  • Forgetting water or minerals when listing all seven components.
  • Giving a food example when the question asks for a function.
