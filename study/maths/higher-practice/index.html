For integers 0≤n≤12, let N=2n+232n+1. Find the sum of all n for which N is a perfect cube and N/3 is a perfect square.

Worked solution

  1. For N to be a cube, n+2 and 2n+1 must both be divisible by 3. This gives n=1,4,7,10.
  2. The prime exponents in N/3 are n+2 and 2n. Both must be even, so n must be even.
  3. The intersection is n=4,10, and their sum is 14.
