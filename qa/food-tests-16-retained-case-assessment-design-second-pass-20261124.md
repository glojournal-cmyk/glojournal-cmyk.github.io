## New assessment-design blockers

- Key positions follow the exact repeating sequence D,C,B,A, four times. Balanced counts alone do not prevent guessing from position. Require verified per-session shuffling of options with correct key mapping, or revise ordering in a held candidate and test it. Do not change historical answers.
- 16/16 cases offer no distractor differing from the key by a single indicator. These items can reward elimination rather than accurate discrimination; before formal import, consider plausible single-reagent interpretation errors while maintaining one complete-set key. Do not claim difficulty or psychometric quality from a distance metric alone.
