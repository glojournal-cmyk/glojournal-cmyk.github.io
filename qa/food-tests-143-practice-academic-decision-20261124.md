## What changed

- Corrected the distinction between repeatability and reproducibility for the faint iodine result. The same pupil repeats the test on a fresh portion under the same conditions.
- Clarified the planned and actual heating times in the Benedict’s comparison, and the volumes to match across the two emulsion-test trials.
- Replaced a duplicate misconception about a green Benedict’s result. Corrected a distractor about ethanol vapour, the wording of two matching tasks and the unsupported claim in the Biuret interpretation question.
- Confirmed 28 legitimate typed forms that lost credit in at least one scorer. Of these, 27 failed in both and one failed in only one. All 28 are covered by the revised answer lists. Examples include “starch not detected”, “milky white” and “a beaker of hot water”. A closed list cannot establish fair marking of every plausible English answer.
