## 61 weak, overly concise original wrong-option explanations repaired as **QA-only wording proposals**

A targeted review found **61** wrong-option error descriptions of fewer than 25 characters across **22** Simultaneous Equations questions; examples were “Stop after finding x”, “Reverse the sign of y”, “Omit the y-term” and “Stop at kx”. These often name a mistake without showing that the displayed incorrect value is generated by that mistake.

The separate [61-row proposed explanation register](../qa-staging/math-v314-simultaneous-61-short-distractor-path-repair-register-20261123.json) records original A–F text and actual source x/y values and **reproduces the wrong numeric option by the identified erroneous operation** for each of the 61. It preserves the original source item and its key unchanged.
