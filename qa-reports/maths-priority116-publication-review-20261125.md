## Publication decisions to retain

- The standard-form files 003, 031 and 040 solve the same optimisation structure: start from a baseline and divide spare capacity by the extra cost of a substitution. Use alternative variants for practice. A learner should not earn three independent concept awards for this structure.
- Graphs 025 tests ordering and bounding three linear expressions. Graphs 011 requires percentage comparisons and inequalities. Rearranging 001 also requires solving a rational inequality or equivalent monotonic reasoning. Verify those prerequisites before assigning these as autumn Daily tasks.
- Standard Form 021 asks for a **maximum amount**, not the number of feasible percentages. Its stored intended-AO description still says to count solutions. The student-facing key and worked answer are correct. An accurate editorial description is ‘convert units, determine the feasible integer corrections, and maximise the amount removed.’
- Standard Form 031's F commits two errors and gives fewer than the minimum 750 files. A response of 666 alone cannot isolate which misconception the learner has.
- The exact revisions retain their existing import, Daily and Mastery dispositions. There was no production integration or device test in this review.
