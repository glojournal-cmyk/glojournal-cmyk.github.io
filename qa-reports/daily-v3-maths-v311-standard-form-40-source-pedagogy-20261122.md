## Independently checked structural calculations for the highest-risk families
- **Q41–44:** Sample mass = particle count × mass per particle, divided by supplied volume. Correct densities 300, 400, 500, 600 g/cm³. This is a science/context concern, **not** a key error.
- **Q57–60:** Particle mass \(=2\times 10^{k}\cdot3\times10^{-k+4}=6\times10^4\) grams in each instance; after 25% loss, \(4.5\times10^4\) grams remain. Dividing by \(1.5\times10^2\) or \(1.5\times10^3\) gives 300, 30, 300, 30 g/cm³. **The two pairs repeat exactly the correct answer and core working**.
- **Q69–72:** \((8\times10^k +4\times10^{k-1}-2\times10^{k-1})/(2\times10^6)=4.1\times10^{k-6}\). For k=8..11, answers differ in exponent only, not mantissa or conceptual arithmetic.
- **Q73–76:** \(((6\times10^k+2\times10^{k-1})\cdot0.90)/(5\times10^{k-5})=1.116\times10^5\) g **for every** k=9,10,11,12. Original source changed scale of all quantities together and accidentally preserved an identical correct numeric answer in four supposed separate challenges.
- **Q77–80:** \(N=[T-A]/(0.80\cdot 2\times10^4)\) where T is target total and A added items; independently verifies 3×10⁴, 4×10⁵, 5×10⁶, 6×10⁷. All four still share the same calculation recipe.
