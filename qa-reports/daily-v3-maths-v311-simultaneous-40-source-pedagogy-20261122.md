## Ten original family-specific Academic QA findings
### 1. SIMULTANEOUS-001..004 — `V3-SIM-G7-SOLVE-DERIVED`
**Primary risk:** `EXACT_SYSTEM_DUPLICATED`.

Q161–164 solve a linear system then evaluate x²+y. **Each of the four exact original systems is repeated at Q169–172** (target there is xy), and Q161's exact system is repeated again at Q189 (target 4x+5y). This is not just same concept but literal reuse of givens: 2x+3y=7, 3x−2y=4 gives x=2,y=1. Q161 key C=5; Q169 key C=2; Q189 key A=13. If scheduled near each other, knowing x,y from the first trivialises the latter.

**Status:** NOT SIGNED; individual 5-option plausible misconceptions, school taught and target grade calibration outstanding.
