## Improvement priorities

1. Make Today's work explicit: show the next action, practice/mastery phases, completed counts and saved status in one consistent place. Keep the agreed task targets; avoid silently lowering requirements.
2. Build a focused review queue from actual wrong answers and assessment feedback, linked to the exact topic and concise notes. Distinguish answering enough questions from mastering a topic.
3. Improve save confidence: visible last saved time, clear backup reminders and a recovery checklist. Cross-device cloud synchronization would need a separate design; current storage is per browser/device.
4. Review the French bank's 184 exact-duplicate entries and uneven distractors flagged by the content audit, retaining intentional variations only where they add learning value.
5. Keep the new browser and composed-bank marking checks in CI, tighten the 20 synonym warnings, and centralize runtime release versioning so future edits cannot accidentally import two app instances.
6. Make expired assessment labels clearer and map retired activities visibly to their current equivalents.

## Limits

WebKit at tablet dimensions is not a physical iPad test. The interaction suite waits for finite school-data loading between full-document navigations: forced navigation in automated WebKit reports cancelled fetches as CORS page errors, including caught optional-data requests. Rapid page switching on a physical iPad remains a separate device check. Route smoke checks do not exhaust every possible game session, answer wording, accessibility interaction or AI answer quality. Model-answer checks establish consistency with declared marking rules, not external curriculum certification. Tests use isolated browser storage and do not recover historical work that was never recorded on the daughter's device. The three retired games now redirect to current features rather than restoring their former gameplay.

## Follow-up: Daily vocabulary preference

The two cards in the parent's screenshot are French Daily 30 and Latin Daily 30. They previously used a foundation/grammar/weak-point/production mix despite the vocabulary label. Revision 9fd40e223a2342ec39884ead28b61be82f6fc1ec replaces that mix with actual Year 8 vocabulary concepts: 445 French and 424 Latin words/taught phrases available in the source banks.

- Select 30 distinct word concepts, prefer typed recall where supported, and treat opposite translation directions as the same word.
- Retain the existing target of 30 correct credited answers and existing saved progress.
- Rotate across the available Year 8 word bank; a small first grammar topic no longer constrains Daily vocabulary.
- Prefer fresh/less-seen words and avoid correctly answered words from the previous two days when alternatives exist.
- Reserve up to six initial slots for genuinely due vocabulary reviews; recent correct words can return if the eligible bank is exhausted.
- Use a full date hash instead of adding character codes, avoiding identical seeds for dates with the same digit sum.
- Fast correct Daily vocabulary answers no longer create extra next-day speed-confirmation tasks. Normal scheduled reviews remain.

Some repeated vocabulary is intentional spaced retrieval. The old narrow foundation pool, weak-point priority, fast-answer confirmation and simple date seed could additionally cause repeated material. The daughter's exact historical repeats cannot be attributed to one rule without her device's exported records.
