# Pet artwork quality repair

All twelve companions now use a standalone transparent image for each of their five stages, through `pet/pet-art-hd-20261009.js`. Assets are saved in `pet/art-hd-20261009/level-{1..5}/{species}.webp`.

Pebble Wisp previously used 256px cells and the two hidden pets used 512px cells. Their fifteen replacement sprites were restored with the built-in image generation tool from the approved five-stage sheets, then exported at their native 1254 × 1254 resolution as high-quality WebP with transparency. No artificial upscaling was applied. The nine ordinary companions retain their existing 1254 × 1254 artwork. A small SVG alpha filter smooths matte fringe at display size without blurring the character colors.

The prompt set selected one sheet cell per call (top left, top middle, top right, bottom left, bottom middle for levels 1–5), requesting one standalone transparent sprite, sharper existing details, the same character identity, pose, palette, accessories and evolution stage, and clean edges. Pebble's ivory and gold appearance, the owl's white feathers and gold jewelry, and the spider's purple jewels and eight legs were preserved. Images were assigned by the generation request's level, rather than asynchronous completion order.

The homepage, companion goals and preview panels, pet chooser, current pet, and evolution journal use the shared sprite helper. The old Pebble sheet CSS override was removed. Entry versions were refreshed without changing the React store module URLs.

Validation: all 60 asset mappings and native dimensions; transparent alpha; identical retained ordinary artwork; pet MP, care state, and companion goals regression checks. Evolution costs, wallet, unlocks, and saved pet levels are unchanged.
